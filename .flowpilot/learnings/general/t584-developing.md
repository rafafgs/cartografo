### t584 (developing, unverified)

- index.ts and proposals.ts would be circular if proposals.ts imported extractValue/extractFlag/requireNothingElse from index.ts (index.ts imports runProposals from proposals.ts). Duplicated the three tiny parsing primitives locally in proposals.ts instead of exporting them from index.ts — both files stay within the declared shared-file surface and no circular import is introduced.
- GET /v1/proposals?project_id=<unknown> style filters aside, POST /v1/graphs refuses a repeat of the same document with 409 class_already_registered — a second pending proposal needed for a test (e.g. an unapplied proposal to revert) has to be opened as a second POST /v1/proposals over the SAME graph_id/target_version (with different operations, to dodge the dedupe key), never via a second POST /v1/graphs of the same fixture.
- reject/revert's --reason and approve/apply's lack of one are told apart by whether --reason is even extracted from the argument list before requireNothingElse runs: for approve/apply it is deliberately left in the stream so a stray --reason falls through as an unrecognised extra (the same refusal shape every other subcommand uses), rather than needing a bespoke 'this verb does not take --reason' message.
