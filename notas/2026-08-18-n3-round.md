## Holes found (none became a ticket — Rafael decides)

1. **A quota/rate-limit failure is indistinguishable from a crash.** The adapter sees
   `api_error_status: 429`, `terminal_reason: api_error`, and the reset time in the message,
   and reports `failure_kind: null`; the runner re-leases in 3 s; three attempts burn in
   20 seconds and t265 blocks the job. Sessions do survive (nothing is lost; `unblock`
   resumes) but they do not resume by themselves. Fix candidates: `failure_kind: quota` from
   the adapter; the runner does not count it toward the cap and waits until the reset (or an
   exponential backoff) before re-leasing.
2. **The collection node is the cost of the graph**: 25–45 minutes and 4 M cache-read tokens
   per run for this thesis (EDGAR filings, precedents, tax rules). It is what the flow
   surveyor's proposal 1 of round 3 attacks (closed research brief with a stop criterion);
   until a proposal like that is applied and measured, every traversal of this class costs
   US$ 5–13 and most of it here.
3. **The triage asks whether the thesis is inside the circle** when the circle text does not
   literally name the asset class — a fair question, but on an unattended run it is a gate
   that needs a person; the answer belongs in `project.circulo_de_competencia` (name the
   class) or in the triage instructions (how to judge "instance of").
4. **The 16 KB input-values cap** (round 3, hole 1) still stands and would have hit the
   analysis/red-team of every run of this round.
5. Driver defect (operator's tool, not the product): one canned answer for every question;
   fixed to answer per node before the resume.
