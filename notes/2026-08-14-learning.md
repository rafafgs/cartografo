# What characterises learning here (2026-08-14)

An iteration with Rafael on three questions: what "learning" means here, are
gates skills?, and where the learnings are recorded.

## Gates are skills

Everything that executes in the system is a skill with a contract; what changes
is the role (doing, checking, routing). A nuance settled: a gate is
**deterministic whenever possible** (running tests, validating a schema,
building) and **agentic only where there is judgement** ("were the acceptance
criteria met?"). The gate's contract: input = the artifact + the ticket's
criteria; output = passed/failed/escalates, always with evidence attached. An
agentic gate verifies with evidence of its own (it runs the result), not with the
report of whoever did the work.

## The definition of learning

**Learning = a versioned diff on a learnable surface, with evidence from the log,
approved at a human gate.** No model weights, no in-flight adaptation. Four
learnable surfaces:

1. **Topology** — nodes, edges, order (e.g. t81's bottleneck of 41 minutes of
   human waiting → the proposal "concentrate dependency questions in refine").
2. **Gates** — criteria tightened or loosened. Every defect that escaped becomes
   a future verification (the testers' 6 tickets = 6 candidate checks for the
   develop gate). Probably the most valuable surface.
3. **The nodes' skills** — instructions amended (e.g. copyright steering becomes
   a permanent line in the refine skill).
4. **Policies** — timeouts, concurrency, auto-answering by class of question on
   the basis of precedent.

## Where it is recorded: three mechanisms, all in the database

- **The book of proposals.** The topografo (the final node of every execution)
  issues proposals: the target artifact + version, the diff, evidence from the
  log and **the metric it expects to move**. A proposal is a hypothesis;
  approval is an experiment; the next round's telemetry records the result on the
  proposal (confirmed / no effect / worse → revert). A consolidated learning = a
  confirmed hypothesis. A rejected proposal stays as negative knowledge (do not
  re-propose).
- **Version chains.** Graph, contract, skill and policy are versioned; the chain
  of diffs with their justifications answers "why is the graph like this?" with a
  log, not with archaeology.
- **A base of precedents.** Answered questions become consultable precedent: it
  feeds auto-answering and gives the synthesizer context for the graphs of
  similar classes.

## The strategic consequence

Because the learning lives in versioned artifacts (not in weights and not in the
context of a session), **it survives a change of engine**. The summarising
sentence: the system gets more intelligent without any model getting more
intelligent.
